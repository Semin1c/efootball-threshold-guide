02 / KNOW THE LIMITS

Targets are a starting XI.
Evidence is the referee.

Build for the role

Ranges are this guide’s heuristic starting points, not official recommendations or measured minimums. You will rarely hit every upper bound on one card. Prioritize the actions you actually use, then test the trade-offs.

Compare the final values shown in your current Game Plan, including active manager and booster effects. Record condition, stamina and triggered skills separately. These are attribute ratings, not progression-point allocations.

Engine updates reset assumptions

This review uses v6.0.0 as its gameplay baseline. KONAMI changed movement and goalkeeper animations. An old test can be useful without remaining valid on today’s build. Mobile, console and PC equivalence is not assumed.

“No hard threshold” means none established here, not that a stat is useless or perfectly linear. A higher rating can still help beyond a target.

How evidence earns a label

No gameplay experiment was performed for this guide. Sources were reviewed on . Current-test means a traceable current-engine test, with its scope preserved; it does not mean independently reproduced on every platform.

To promote a claim, record the exact version and platform, player model, manager, boosters, form, stamina, skills, inputs, repeated trials and measurement uncertainty. Change one stat at a time. Check values on both sides of the proposed breakpoint and seek independent replication. Retain corrections and contradictions.

All positional ranges are editorial. A source attached to a mechanic does not validate the range next to it. Weak-foot traits, form, injury resistance, height and skills are considered separately from progression targets.

How future updates work

A weekly GitHub job checks vetted public pages and the Amadeusz feed. Text matching adds possible mechanics findings to the provisional feed with their original source and discovery date. It does not independently verify a test or infer a universal numerical threshold.

Existing recommendations remain unchanged. Matching source revisions retain links to previous entries; duplicates are skipped. Failed fetches preserve previous findings and are shown under Source availability. The reviewed chart date and automatic check date are kept separate.

Checks run on Mondays at 14:17 UTC and can also be started manually in GitHub Actions. The source window is bounded, so some posts and image/video-only findings may be missed. Promotion into the reviewed chart still requires evidence review.
